# Enterprise Cybersecurity Risk Management Program
### Building a Cyber Risk Program from Inception to Executive Reporting

## Project Overview
This simulated project demonstrates how I would **design, build, and operationalize an Enterprise Cybersecurity Risk Management Program from the ground up** for an organization without a centralized cyber risk capability.

The project demonstrates governance, policy, risk methodology, assessment, treatment, remediation monitoring, continuous monitoring, and executive reporting.

> **Portfolio Notice:** All organizations, systems, risks, and data used in this project are fictional or sanitized.

## Program Transformation
**Inception State:** Cybersecurity risks are identified across vulnerability management, cloud security, IAM, third-party assessments, audits, compliance, incidents, and policy exceptions, but are tracked inconsistently with no centralized methodology, ownership model, or executive reporting.

**Completion State:** A structured cyber risk program is established with defined governance, standardized assessment, centralized risk tracking, defined ownership, treatment and acceptance workflows, continuous monitoring, and executive reporting.

# Program Build

## Phase 1 – Foundation & Governance
Established the foundation required to operate a consistent and accountable cyber risk management program.

**Built:** Current-State Cyber Risk Assessment • Cybersecurity Risk Management Policy • Cyber Risk Governance/RACI • Risk Appetite & Tolerance Criteria • Risk Taxonomy • Risk Scoring Methodology • 5×5 Risk Matrix

**Outcome:** Established governance, accountability, risk boundaries, categorization, and assessment criteria for the program.

## Phase 2 – Risk Assessment & Treatment
Designed the process for converting cybersecurity findings into documented and managed enterprise risks.

**Built:** Risk Intake Workflow • Enterprise Cyber Risk Register • Inherent Risk Assessment • Control Effectiveness Assessment • Residual Risk Methodology • Risk Treatment & Acceptance Workflow

**Outcome:** Created a repeatable process for identifying, assessing, prioritizing, owning, and treating cybersecurity risks.

## Phase 3 – Operationalization & Monitoring
Established processes for continuously operating and monitoring the cyber risk program.

**Built:** Remediation Tracker • Risk Ownership Process • Remediation Target Timelines • Escalation Workflow • Risk Review Cadence • Continuous Monitoring

**Outcome:** Enabled ongoing accountability, remediation tracking, escalation, reassessment, and risk monitoring.

## Phase 4 – Executive Reporting & Oversight
Converted cybersecurity risk data into management-level visibility and decision support.

**Built:** Cybersecurity KPIs • Cybersecurity KRIs • Enterprise Risk Heat Map • Risk Aging Analysis • Executive Risk Dashboard

**Outcome:** Provided leadership with visibility into risk exposure, remediation performance, overdue risks, residual risk, and trends.

## Program Outcome
**Before:** Fragmented Tracking → Inconsistent Assessment → Unclear Ownership → Limited Monitoring → Limited Executive Visibility

**After:** Governance → Centralized Intake → Standardized Assessment → Defined Ownership → Treatment → Remediation → Continuous Monitoring → Executive Reporting

The completed environment demonstrates how cybersecurity findings can be transformed into **documented, owned, measurable, and actionable business risk decisions**.
